# Data and assets

Default simulation, transaction, learner, inventory, leaky-cohort, audio,
supply and engagement examples are authored synthetic demonstrations
generated by the included code with recorded seeds. These are distributed
under the code's MIT licence. Original geometric meme illustrations and
captions generated by `lab/memes.py` are dedicated under CC0 1.0.

Research Thunderdome loads the breast-cancer and wine benchmarks from the
installed scikit-learn package. Their provenance is in each dataset's `DESCR`
field, and their original provider terms apply. The breast-cancer dataset is
used only as a modelling benchmark, not for medical diagnosis.

External dependencies and user uploads retain their own terms. No scraped
platform corpus, private receipt, real transaction database, or personal
learner history is bundled. Do not publish your runtime directory.


## V2 interface assets

- `assets/cover.svg`, interface icons, spatial scenes and code-native graphics are authored for this release and distributed with the source licence.
- `web/font.ttf` and `web/font-bold.ttf` are unmodified Liberation Sans font software, distributed under SIL OFL 1.1. Their copyright and full licence are retained in `web/FONT-LICENSE.txt`. Their licence is separate from the code licence.
- External design references informed composition only; their screenshots, illustrations, logos and code are not bundled.
- Demo clips use captures of this original local interface and the project’s own demonstration data.
